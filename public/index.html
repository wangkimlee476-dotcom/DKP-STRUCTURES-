Silver Plan and above — proposed future share-allocation programme

DKP STRUCTURES intends to develop a proposed future share-allocation programme beginning with the Silver Plan and extending to Gold, Platinum, Diamond and Founding levels. Contributions specifically accepted and recorded under the approved programme may be considered for future share allocation when the company becomes operational.

Important — this programme is not yet open and no shares are currently being offered or issued. Reaching or being recognised at Silver level or above does not by itself grant shares or create an automatic entitlement. Only contributions expressly accepted and recorded under definitive written programme terms, after the required company approvals and South Korean legal and regulatory review, may qualify.

The final documents must explain the eligibility rules, share class, company valuation and conversion formula, calculation date, allocation timing, shareholder rights, applicable fees or taxes, transfer restrictions and what happens if shares cannot lawfully be issued. Any allocation remains subject to applicable South Korean laws, required corporate approvals and any cross-border offering restrictions.

Until the programme is formally approved and opened, all existing contributions remain under the current voluntary supporter arrangement. They do not create shares, ownership, voting rights, dividends, repayment rights or guaranteed returns. Basic and Bronze contributions are outside this proposed share-allocation programme unless future definitive terms expressly provide otherwise.

Safeguards before any share issue

  • Confirm the company’s legal incorporation and existing share capital.
  • Prepare and document a defensible company valuation and fully diluted share count.
  • Approve the share class, price, eligibility rules and maximum dilution.
  • Obtain required corporate approvals and South Korean securities-law advice, including cross-border review.
  • Issue shares only through formal subscription documents and official corporate records.
